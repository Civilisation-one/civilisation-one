## Role
Agents extend this node without breaking physical invariants.

## Hard Constraints
- Preserve mass conservation
- Preserve free-energy monotonicity
- Do not introduce non-local interactions unless explicitly defined

## Extension Rules
- New physics = new module, not modification of core
- Quantum layer must remain unitary
- All additions require a verification test

## Allowed Directions
- Higher dimensions (2D, 3D)
- Coupled fields
- External constant calibration (EM spectrum, α, etc.)
